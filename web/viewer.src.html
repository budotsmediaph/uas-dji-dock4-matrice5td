Lead screw
0 / 185 mm

The aircraft's battery bay and the four compartments lie on one circle (radius 165 mm, estimated) around a rotor axis parallel to the aircraft. The cradle turns with the rotor; its hook rides a lead screw along that axis to pull or push a battery. The hatch plate opposite the cradle closes the pad when the rotor is home.

Batteries 4 packs, 3 stored at most

IDWhereLevelState

One compartment always stays free so the rotor can put the used battery down before it fetches a charged one. Two packs charge at a time (27 min, 15 → 95 %). Levels in the clip are set at the start; the LED patterns follow user manual p. 71.

    What the model is based on

    • DJIClosed 639 × 640 × 836 mm, open 1750 × 640 × 550 mm, 4 compartments holding at most 3 batteries, 2 charging channels, swap under 25 s, battery 172.9 × 96.02 × 67.7 mm. The battery leaves through the tail; the aircraft stays powered by the pad's wireless coil.
    • DJIPart numbering: dock items 1–14 (user manual p. 24), aircraft items (p. 10), battery LED bands (p. 71), footprint 567 × 555 mm on a 400 × 400 mm bolt pattern (installation manual p. 44).
    • FittedThe aircraft. DJI's Matrice 5TD STEP is cut off on DJI's server before any face was written, but its 95,153 vertex points survive in assembly coordinates. Fuselage sections, motor centres, battery bay, gimbal and LiDAR are fitted to them.
    • FittedSwap layout: a rotor on an axis parallel to the aircraft, a C-shaped cage with compartments at about 10:30, 9, 7 and 5 o'clock seen from behind, a cradle with a lead screw and hook. Read from DJI's launch film (62–77 s), Drone DR1's render and KOSECO's footage of the cradle rising behind the H marker.
    • EstimateTransfer radius 165 mm, rotor axis 320 mm above the ground, hook stroke 185 mm, screw pitch 8 mm, hinge positions, hump profile of the pad, the hatch plate opposite the cradle, and the split of the 25 s into 15 steps.
